# Edo Moon: mission, vision, values

Approved by the owner on 2026-10-25 (Paperclip EDO-9, drafted by ceo v3).
The sources of each line and nine open questions are in EDO-9's `vision`
document; the questions are the owner's to answer, and nothing here decides
them. The brand rules (`BRAND_RULES.md`) outrank this page.

## Mission

Make Edo Moon a quiet daily place for the cards: grow its readers through
social media and search, keep the site and the readings good, then find
sustainable revenue for me to decide on, without ever breaking the brand
rules.

(Widened from marketing to the product by the owner on 2026-10-27; the
product is proposed by the CEO and built by Claude Code, brand rule 12.)

## Vision

1. Edo Moon is a quiet place to stop for a moment: you ask a question
   plainly, draw from a ukiyo-e deck, and get a reading that names your
   situation and one small thing you can do today.
2. It is free, needs no account, and keeps as little about you as it can.
3. It is for adults, for reflection and entertainment, and it says openly
   that the readings are AI-written and the art is made with generative
   tools.
4. Over the next year or two it should become a daily habit for a steady
   group of readers, who find it through its own posts and through search
   and come back for the card of the day and their journal, with the art and
   the voice as the reason they come.
5. Revenue, if any, comes after that, in a form the owner chooses, and only
   if it keeps the cards in front.

## Values

Each line ends with how to check it.

- **V1. Care first, then honesty, then the voice.** Check: every crisis
  message gets the fixed care text, never a model's or an agent's own words,
  and the owner is told at once.
- **V2. Honest about what it is.** Check: bios carry the disclosure line
  word for word, every AI-made post carries the platform's AI label, and no
  public line predicts a fact ("you will") or claims the art is hand-made.
- **V3. The art and the theme lead.** Check: when a choice trades the cards'
  place for more money or more features, the cards win, or the choice goes
  to the owner.
- **V4. Free first, no account.** Check: no payment is switched on and no
  sign-up is asked for until the owner decides.
- **V5. Know as little about readers as we can.** Check: counts stay
  cookieless, no addresses are kept for statistics, questions are deleted
  after 30 days, and we say "landings", never "visitors".
- **V6. One promise, kept every day.** Check: each card of the day carries
  one card, one practical reflection and one action of fifteen words or
  fewer that is never a purchase, a risk, or something needing another
  person's consent.

## What it is not

- **N1. Not a fortune teller or an adviser.** No predictions, no medical,
  legal, financial or pregnancy advice, and no reading of what another
  person feels or will do.
- **N2. Not for minors.** Nothing aimed at anyone under 18.
- **N3. Not an engagement machine.** No luck traded for likes, follows or
  shares; no asking for a word or emoji in the comments; no "ask again until
  it says yes"; never in a hurry to be liked.
- **N4. Not a deck catalogue or a network of sites.** Only Edo Moon. The
  other four decks get no work and no spending.
- **N5. Not a person pretending, and not a salesperson in your messages.**
  Replies stay human until the owner decides otherwise; the account never
  writes first in private messages and never asks for payment there.
