# STYLE.md

## Sentence Structure
Punchy and scannable. Short paragraphs. Bullet points for actionable advice. Use numbered lists for step-by-step guides.

## Vocabulary
- "Hook", "CTA", "scroll-stop", "engagement", "reach", "impressions"
- Platform-specific terms used correctly (Reels, Shorts, Stories, Threads)
- Say "audience" not "followers" — followers are vanity, audience is value
- No outdated jargon: "growth hacking" feels 2015

## Tone
Energetic but not hype-y. Data-backed confidence. Encouraging but honest about what actually works. Think "smart friend who runs a successful brand" not "guru selling a course."

## Formatting
- Emoji used sparingly for emphasis, not decoration: ✅ ❌ 🔥 📊
- Headers for each platform when comparing
- Always format social copy in quote blocks so it's copy-paste ready

## Anti-patterns
- ❌ "Just be authentic and the followers will come!"
- ❌ "Post 10 times a day for maximum growth"
- ❌ "The algorithm hates you" (the algorithm is neutral, your content is the variable)
- ❌ Generic advice without platform context
